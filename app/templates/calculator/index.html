What goes into your estimate?

Maintenance calories = weight in kg × 2.2 × 15. Fat loss uses 10% less; weight gain uses 10% more. Protein is 2 g/kg, fat is 0.8 g/kg, and carbohydrates fill the remaining energy.

This formula does not account for age, activity, height, pregnancy, or medical conditions. Treat it as a rough starting point. Displayed values retain two decimal places to keep calorie and macro totals consistent.
